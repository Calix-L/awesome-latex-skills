# Section Anatomy: Writing Conventions by Section

Use the article type, existing organization and verified target instructions.
These are questions for editing a section, not a mandatory manuscript outline.
A theory paper, dataset description, replication, review and systems paper need
different evidence and structure. Do not generate a missing result, citation,
limitation or ethics statement to fill a template.

## Abstract

Make the central question, approach, supported finding and scope understandable
without requiring the full paper. Preserve what the study actually establishes:
a theorem need not have an accuracy score, and a qualitative study need not have
a numerical improvement. Include supplied quantitative results when they clarify
the central claim; do not borrow example values.

Follow the article type's word limit, structure and citation policy. A short
abstract cannot summarize every detail. Check that it agrees with the results
and retains necessary conditions or qualifications.

## Introduction

Explain the problem, why it matters for the stated context, relevant prior work
and the contribution. A funnel structure is one option, not a fixed sequence or
sentence quota. Preserve the difference between an unresolved problem and a
known problem studied under new conditions.

Make contribution statements specific to the manuscript's evidence. A replication
can contribute validation or a documented discrepancy without claiming a novel
algorithm. Do not add “first”, “state of the art” or a research gap by inference.

## Related Work

Choose thematic, chronological or another organization that explains the relevant
literature. A historical review can legitimately use chronology. A paragraph
does not need a fixed number of citations or an allegation that earlier work
is flawed.

Check that comparisons use the cited work's actual conditions and findings.
Retain complementary work and competing explanations. An omission is a review
question, not permission to fabricate a citation.

## Method

Clarify the formulation, assumptions, notation, components and procedure relevant
to the contribution. Separate training from inference when the study has both;
do not impose those headings on a proof or an observational study.

Retain the details needed to understand and reproduce the work, including
deviations from a standard method. Citing a standard component does not justify
deleting implementation choices that affect results. Ask for missing details
rather than inventing a plausible configuration.

## Experimental Setup

For empirical work, check the data/splits, preprocessing, metrics, comparison
protocol, selection/tuning procedure and resources that affect interpretation.
Report seeds, repetitions, uncertainty, versions or hardware when relevant and
supplied. A GPU specification is not a universal requirement.

Distinguish details absent from the manuscript from details the author actually
did not use. A complete paragraph alone cannot guarantee exact reproduction.

## Results

Lead with the actual finding and connect it to a table, figure, equation or
other evidence. Keep metric direction, denominator, comparison conditions,
uncertainty and negative or null results. “Outperforms all baselines” applies
only if that comparison is established for the stated scope.

Explain a pattern when supported, but distinguish observation from explanation.
An ablation can inform a component's contribution; a mechanism or causal claim
may need additional evidence. Do not add a causal connector merely to improve flow.

## Discussion

Interpret the finding within its demonstrated scope and compare it with prior
evidence. Keep competing explanations and uncertainty. Broader applicability
should be an inference with its basis, not an automatic extension to other
datasets, populations or settings.

A separate Discussion section may be unnecessary for some article types.
Reorganize only within the requested editing intensity and scope.

## Limitations

Describe actual scope boundaries, assumptions, evidence gaps or resource
constraints. Preserve limitations that change how a finding can be interpreted.
Do not invent weaknesses or generic caveats to populate a section.

The requirement, heading and placement depend on the verified venue/year/track/
stage. Suggest missing information for author review when necessary.

## Appendix

Use appendices or supplements for supporting material allowed by the target.
Retain enough main-text information to assess the central claims; do not move
essential evidence solely to evade a page limit.

Check references to appended material, file placement and any applicable
anonymity rule. Do not anonymize a final or public document by default.

## Conclusion

Restate the contribution and finding without widening their scope. Mention
remaining questions only when supported and useful for this article type.
A fixed number of sentences or a mandatory future-work promise is unnecessary.

## Broader Impact / Ethics

Follow applicable target requirements and the manuscript's actual data,
participants, applications and claims. Preserve supplied disclosures and
approvals; missing details require author input. Do not manufacture consent,
ethics approval, mitigations or an assurance that a method has no risks.

Assess relevant impacts when the task calls for it; generic benefit/harm
paragraphs are not a substitute for the study's evidence or required disclosures.

## Acknowledgments

Credit supplied contributors, funding and resources using the target's convention.
Verify permission and anonymity requirements for the submission stage. Do not
add invented support, assign authorship roles or remove final-version credit
because another venue's review template suppresses acknowledgments.
